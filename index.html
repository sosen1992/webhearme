Privacy Policy

Application: HearMe: ស្តាប់អារម្មណ៍ខ្ញុំ (HearMe)

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

HearMe is designed to provide a secure, comforting, and anonymous space for daily emotional tracking and venting. Your personal privacy, mental well-being, and data security are our highest priorities.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

  • Anonymous Authentication: We do not require or collect your real name, email address, phone number, or password. Users access the app anonymously via Firebase Authentication using randomly generated unique identifiers (Anonymous UIDs) to manage app sessions and access controls.
  • Personal Mood Diary (Local-Only Storage): Your private mood logs, personal thoughts, and journal entries are stored strictly on your local device (using local SQLite storage). This sensitive personal data is never transmitted to, stored on, or accessible by our external servers.
  • Community Posts & Ephemeral Messages: Content submitted to the public venting community or support channels is securely processed using Google Cloud Firestore. To preserve user confidentiality and peace of mind, community posts are automatically purged within 24 hours.
  • Device Identifiers & Advertising Data: We integrate the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic crash data for ad delivery, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes.

2. Data Security and Transmission

All network communications between the mobile application and cloud infrastructure are strictly encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS / TLS). We enforce rigorous security rules on our cloud database to prevent unauthorized access or interception.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Ephemeral Community Data: Public venting messages and community interactions are transient and automatically deleted within 24 hours.
  • Local Diary Data: You have full control over your local diary entries. You can permanently delete any entry or clear the app storage at any time directly through the app interface or by uninstalling the app.
